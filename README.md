# 💊 AI Product Explainer – Pharmacy & Pharmaceutical Distribution Prototype

## Overview

AI Product Explainer is a GenAI-powered pharmacy assistant prototype designed to explain medicine-related information in simple and understandable language.

The application allows users to enter a medicine name and select a preferred language. The AI then generates a simplified explanation of the medicine, including common uses, precautions, possible side effects, and a beginner-friendly summary.

This prototype was developed for a **Pharmacy & Pharmaceutical Distribution GenAI Internship Opportunity**.

---

## Problem Statement

Many pharmacy users and patients struggle to understand medicine-related information because medical explanations are often complex and difficult to understand.

This project aims to make medicine information simpler, more accessible, and easier to understand while maintaining responsible AI safety practices.

---

## Features

✅ Medicine explanation in simple language
✅ Multi-language support (English, Hindi, Telugu)
✅ Easy-to-understand medicine information
✅ Safe medical disclaimers
✅ Beginner-friendly and clean user interface
✅ Responsible AI handling for healthcare information

---

## How It Works

1. User enters the medicine name
2. User selects preferred language
3. AI processes medicine-related information
4. The system generates:

   * What the medicine is commonly used for
   * General precautions
   * Possible side effects
   * Simple explanation for normal users
5. Safety disclaimer is displayed

## Responsible AI & Safety

This project follows responsible AI practices for healthcare-related information.

### Safety Rules

* No medical diagnosis
* No dosage recommendations
* No prescription advice
* No replacement for doctors or pharmacists
* Always includes medical disclaimer

**Disclaimer:**
AI-generated information only. Please consult a doctor or pharmacist before use.
